## Philip Garrison

Twenty-five years in commercial operations, across logistics, wine importing and
AgTech. Now designing, building and running AI systems: prospect research,
qualification, grading, field tools, and the checks that keep them running
correctly over time.

I design these systems, build them with Claude Code, and run them for an oyster
farm, a drone start-up and my own company. The judgment is the part worth looking
at.

**→ [ai-systems-portfolio](https://github.com/philip-abis/ai-systems-portfolio)**:
prospect research across eight countries in the Americas and the whole United
States, with working ledgers of 4,702 and 2,341 companies; a vision-based grading
tool with an eval suite; a field sales app deployed to a non-technical user; and
eleven architectural rules each stated with the specific failure that produced it.

If you read one thing, read
[ARCHITECTURE.md](https://github.com/philip-abis/ai-systems-portfolio/blob/main/ARCHITECTURE.md).
Eleven rules, eleven incidents. The day six separate code paths each silently
overrode a human judgment, the time I specified how many prospects the shortlist
should hold instead of what qualified one, and the extraction that returned three
confident figures read off a page that did not exist.

Most of my repositories are private because they hold client and family data.
The portfolio is the machinery, with the identities taken out and the logic left
in.

Fluent English, Spanish and Portuguese. BA Economics, University of Pennsylvania.
